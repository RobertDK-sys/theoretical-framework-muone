# TheoreticalFramework

Formal development of the leptonic-compactness framework that connects the muon
magnetic anomaly to a propulsive velocity law for charged leptons.

## What is here

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| `Basic` | Effective radius `r_ℓ = κ/m_ℓ`, Casimir energy of the internal geometry, the quintic law `E_C ∝ m⁵`, the fifth-power amplification ratio, and the parameter-freedom of the ratio |
| `Leptons` | Lepton mass ratios `207`, `3477` checked against the tabulated masses; Casimir amplifications `(207)⁵ ≈ 4 × 10¹¹` and `(3477)⁵ ≈ 5.1 × 10¹⁷`; the equivalent-field exponent `3` and the plate-volume exponent `2` |
| `Dimensions` | Generalized dimension `n = 5` read off the quintic dependence, the velocity law `vf² = v₂²·(m_ℓ/m₀)^(n−2)` for arbitrary `n`, generalized-unit bookkeeping, the scale substitution `q = m_ℓ/m₀` |
| `Velocity` | Amplification factors `207³ ≈ 8.9 × 10⁶`, `3477³ ≈ 4.2 × 10¹⁰`; ejection velocities with exact intervals; specific impulse; the subluminal/superluminal boundary; the source text's tauonic figures and their `√10` slip |
| `Regimes` | The two regimes as a structure, tiling and nesting of their input windows, continuity, multiplicativity of the amplification, the thrust–power tradeoff, and the unproven empirical thrust question |

## Main results

* `casimirEnergy_eq_quintic` — `E_C = m⁵/κ³`.
* `casimirEnergy_ratio` — `E_C(m₁)/E_C(m₂) = (m₁/m₂)⁵`.
* `CasimirAnsatz.energy_ratio_parameter_free` — the ratio is independent of `κ`,
  of the plate-area coefficient and of the Casimir prefactor.
* `casimir_amplification_muon_general`, `casimir_amplification_tauon_general`.
* `velocity_law` — `vf² = v₂²·r^(n−2)` for every `n`; `velocity_law_five` at `n = 5`.
* `muonAmplification_eq`, `tauonAmplification_eq`.
* `muonMaxEjection_bounds`, `muonMaxEjection_km_per_s` — `≈ 20 700 km/s`.
* `tauonMaxEjection_bounds` — `4.2403 × 10¹² m/s`, and
  `source_tauon_max_velocity_underestimates` for the printed `1.34 × 10¹²`.
* `regimes_tile`, `chain_closes`, `tauonic_window_nested_in_output`,
  `regime_continuity`.
* `thrust_to_power` — `T/P = 2/v_e`; `thrust_not_determined` — the geometry does
  not fix thrust; `ThrustOptimizationConjecture` — recorded, not proved.

## Building

The development depends only on Mathlib.  In this machine Mathlib is prebuilt at
`/opt/mathlib` and on `LEAN_PATH`, so the supported way to check the sources is

```
make verify
```

which elaborates each module with `lean` in dependency order into
`.lake/build/lib/lean/`.  A `lakefile.toml` is provided for a checkout that has a
writable Mathlib; `lake build` is the same check done by Lake.

The report in `paper/` is generated from these sources by
`python3 paper/tools/build_paper.py`, which re-runs the verification, extracts
every declaration and its statement, recomputes every numerical constant
independently, and typesets the result with LaTeX.
